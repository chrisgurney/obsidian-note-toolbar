# Code of Conduct

Note Toolbar is a community project, and everyone who uses it or contributes to it is welcome here. This code applies to everyone in the project's spaces: issues, pull requests, and Discussions in this repository.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being welcoming and respectful, including to new contributors
- Giving and receiving feedback graciously
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the project
- Showing empathy towards other community members

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, intimidation, or threats, in public or in private
- Insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Publishing other people's private information without their permission
- Sustained disruption of discussion, or other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

If you experience or witness behaviour that violates this code, please report it privately to the maintainer, using the [Note Toolbar Feedback form](https://forms.gle/sa75KNAQiWTtAY4P8). Reports are handled confidentially.

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code, and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with it. The maintainer may also temporarily or permanently ban a contributor for behaviour that is not aligned with this code.

## Support

Note Toolbar is maintained by one person, in their spare time. There is no formal support SLA: reviews happen when the maintainer can get to them, and the same applies to issue triage.

- Questions and feature requests belong in [Discussions](https://github.com/chrisgurney/obsidian-note-toolbar/discussions).
- Bugs belong in [issues](https://github.com/chrisgurney/obsidian-note-toolbar/issues).
- Security vulnerabilities are handled separately; see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
